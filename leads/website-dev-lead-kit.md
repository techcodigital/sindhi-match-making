# Website Development Leads — Hunting Kit
**Banaya:** 21 Oct 2026 · **Kis liye:** har roz naye "website chahiye" waale leads dhoondhna aur unhe client banana

---

## 0. Pehle sach — 3 line mein

1. **Marketplace leads (Freelancer/Upwork) sabse aasan hain, par sabse ganda paisa.** Live check kiya: website-design category mein 426 open projects, aur ek ₹12,500 ke project par 339 bids. Yahan aap price pe ladte ho, value pe nahi.
2. **LinkedIn / Facebook / Google ke posts sabse garam hote hain** — client khud "chahiye" bol raha hota hai, koi middleman nahi. Par ye platforms login ke peeche hain, isliye inke liye maine **exact search URLs + daily routine** diya hai (section 2), jisse aap 15 minute mein din ke fresh posts nikal lo.
3. **Sabse zyada paisa wahan hai jahan client ne abhi tak post hi nahi daala** — aisa business jiske paas website hi nahi hai (section 3). Indore mein ye aapke ghar ke aas-paas hazaron mein hain. Competition: zero.

**Rule:** din ke 2 ghante = 40 min marketplace + 40 min social posts + 40 min direct (no-website) outreach. 30 din mein 3-5 paying clients realistic hai, agar follow-up chhod nahi rahe.

## 3. Sabse zyada converting leads: "jiske paas website hi nahi hai"

Client jo post daal raha hai, uska budget already ghisa hua hai. Client jiske paas website nahi hai — usko problem ka pata bhi nahi, aur woh aapki poori price deta hai.

### 3.1 Google Maps method (20 minute, 15 leads)

1. Google Maps kholo → `dentist in Indore` (ya jo bhi niche).
2. Listings par click karo. Jiske paas **"Website" button nahi hai** aur sirf phone/WhatsApp hai — **woh HOT lead hai**.
3. Google Maps se hi phone number aur "Photos" dekh lo (unka business real hai ya nahi).
4. Ek Excel/CSV mein naam, phone, area, rating, reviews count likho.
5. Din ke 10 businesses → 4 call connect → 1 meeting. Ye normal ratio hai.

**Indore ke best niches (inke paas website hoti hi nahi, aur budget hota hai):**

| Niche | Kyun website chahiye | Package idea |
|---|---|---|
| Dentists / skin clinics | Patients Google karte hain, appointment form | ₹15k–25k + booking |
| Coaching institutes | Result, batch, fee, admission form | ₹15k–30k |
| Banquet halls / wedding planners | Photos gallery, date availability, enquiry | ₹20k–40k |
| Interior designers / architects | Portfolio hi bikri hai | ₹25k–50k |
| Gyms / yoga studios | Membership plans, trial booking | ₹15k–25k |
| Jewellers / saree & boutique | Catalogue, WhatsApp order | ₹20k–40k |
| CA / advocate / property dealers | Trust + service pages + Google par dikhna | ₹15k–25k |
| Restaurants / cafés | Menu, table booking, Zomato se independent | ₹15k–30k |

### 3.2 Instagram-only business method

Search: `#indorecafe`, `#indoreboutique`, `#indorebakery`, `#indoreinteriors`, `#indorephotographer`.
Bio mein link dekho:
- Sirf `wa.me/...` link → **website nahi hai** = lead.
- `linktr.ee` → **website nahi hai** = lead.
- Unki site hai par mobile pe tooti hui → **redesign lead**.
DM ka template section 5 mein hai.

### 3.3 "Purani website" waale (redesign = fast money)

- Site kholo phone pe. Agar 3 second mein load na ho, ya text chhota ho, ya "© 2019" likha ho → redesign pitch.
- Google PageSpeed Insights (`pagespeed.web.dev`) se score nikaal ke screenshot bhejo. **"Aapki site 34/100 hai, customers ja rahe hain"** — ye line sabse zyada reply laati hai.

### 3.4 Aapka unfair advantage (Sindhi community)

Aapke paas already ek **matrimonial platform** ka working product hai. Isko niche banao:

- **Marriage bureaus / matchmakers** → apna branded matrimonial portal (aapka ready code base)
- **Sindhi panchayat / community associations** → member directory, events, notices, donation
- **Sindhi temples / dharamshalas** → donation page + event calendar + gallery
- **Community banquet halls & caterers** → booking + gallery
- **Sindhi business families** → family business website + next-gen branding

Ye audience trust pe chalti hai. Ek reference mil gaya toh 5 aur aa jaate hain. Indore ke alawa Bhopal, Jaipur, Surat, Ahmedabad, Ulhasnagar, Pune — Sindhi business hubs hain, remote delivery chalegi.

## 5. Outreach scripts (copy-paste, Hinglish)

### 5.1 LinkedIn — comment pehle, DM baad mein

Comment (post pe, 5 minute ke andar):
> "Sir, isme ek cheez add karni chahiye — Google par 'near me' search mein aane ke liye location + service pages. Maine [similar business] ke liye banaya tha, wahan enquiries 2x hui thi. All the best!"

DM (comment ke 10 min baad):
> "Hi [Name], aapke post pe comment kiya tha. Main Indore se hoon, websites banata hoon — mainly clinics, coaching aur local business ke liye (booking form + WhatsApp + Google par ranking).
>
> Aapke liye ek chhota kaam free mein kar sakta hoon: 1-page layout mockup, aapke business ke naam se. 2 din mein bhej dunga. Agar pasand aaye toh aage baat karenge, warna wo bhi aapka.
>
> Interested?"

### 5.2 WhatsApp (no-website business ko)

> "Namaste [Name] ji, main [Aapka naam], Indore se. Aapka [business] Google Maps par dekha — reviews achhe hain par website nahi hai, isliye jab koi '[service] near me' search karta hai toh aap list mein nahi aate.
>
> Main [niche] ke liye website banata hoon — enquiry form, WhatsApp button, Google par dikhna. 7 din mein ready, ₹[X] se shuru. Meri ek sample site: [link]
>
> 5 minute baat kar sakte hain? Ya main aapko 2 sample bhej doon?"

### 5.3 Cold call (30 second, yaad kar lo)

> "Namaste, main [naam] bol raha hoon, Indore se. Aapke [business] ki website nahi hai na? — [pause] — Main isi kaam ke liye call kar raha hoon. Aapke jaise [niche] ke liye main website banata hoon jo Google par aata hai aur WhatsApp enquiry deta hai. 7 din mein ban jaati hai.
>
> Main aaj aapko WhatsApp par 2 sample bhej deta hoon, aap dekh lijiye. Aapka WhatsApp isi number par hai?"

### 5.4 Freelancer.com proposal (Hinglish, short)

> "Namaste [Name], brief poora padha.
>
> Aapko chahiye: [3 points unke brief se copy karo — yahi trick sabse zyada kaam karti hai].
>
> Main [X] saal se websites banata hoon, aapke jaisa [niche] ka kaam kiya hai: [1 link].
>
> Plan: Day 1-2 design, Day 3-5 development, Day 6 testing, Day 7 handover + 1 video jisme main aapko sikhata hoon ki content khud kaise badalna hai.
>
> ₹[X] fixed, 7 din, 2 revision free. Domain-hosting aapke naam par hoga.
>
> Ek chhoti si baat — aapke brief mein [ek genuine observation/question] clear nahi tha. Bata dijiye toh main aaj hi shuru kar sakta hoon."

**Bid price rule:** average bid ka **70–80%** rakho, aur **pehle 10 bidders** mein raho. Sabse sasta mat bano — naye clients sasta dekh ke kaam ka quality doubt karte hain.

### 5.5 Instagram DM

> "Hi! Aapka [product] bahut achha lag raha hai. Ek cheez notice ki — bio mein sirf WhatsApp link hai, website nahi. Isse Google par aapki shop nahi aati, sirf Instagram followers tak pahunchti hai.
>
> Main aise brands ke liye chhoti website banata hoon — catalogue + WhatsApp order + Google Maps. 5 din, ₹[X]. Sample bhejun?"

### 5.6 Follow-up (day 3 & day 7 — yahan 60% deals banti hain)

Day 3:
> "[Name] ji, kal wala sample bhej raha hoon — [1 line]. Dekh lijiye, koi pressure nahi. 🙂"

Day 7:
> "[Name] ji, main is week 2 hi project le raha hoon, isliye pooch raha hoon. Aapke liye slot rakhu ya hold kar doon?"

## 6. Lead scoring — 10 second mein decide karo

| Signal | + / − |
|---|---|
| Khud "chahiye" likha hai | +3 |
| Business ka naam/address public hai (real hai) | +2 |
| Website nahi hai ya kharab hai | +2 |
| Recent posts / active Instagram (paisa chal raha hai) | +2 |
| Phone number diya hua hai | +2 |
| Budget mention hai | +1 |
| 100+ bids / "free demo chahiye" | −3 |
| "Equity ke liye kaam karo" / "exposure milega" | −5 |
| Sirf email, no phone, no business name | −2 |

**8+ = aaj hi call. 5–7 = WhatsApp + follow-up. 4 se neeche = chhod do.**

## 8. Red flags (paisa aur time bachao)

- Client bolta hai **"platform ke bahar WhatsApp karo"** freelancer par → account ban + scam.
- **"Pehle free demo banao, phir decide karenge"** → mostly free kaam karwa rahe hote hain. Max 1 screenshot/1-page mockup do, poora site nahi.
- **Advance 0%** → hamesha 40–50% advance. Domain/hosting client ke naam par, apne card se mat khareedo.
- **"Payment baad mein, trust karo"** → escrow ya advance, warna nahi.
- WhatsApp pe **"sir aapka number kaise mila"** waala defensive reply → normal hai, script 5.2 use karo, phone mat kaato.
- Website ka **"guaranteed #1 on Google"** promise mat karo — aap pakde jaoge.
