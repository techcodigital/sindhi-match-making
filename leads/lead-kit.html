1Sach pehle: kahan se paisa aata hai

Marketplace = volume, low price, zyada competition. Social posts = garam leads par login-gated. Local businesses = zero competition, sabse zyada price. Teeno ko mix karo, ek par depend mat karo.

Marketplace volume

Freelancer / Truelancer / Upwork. Clients ready hote hain, par 100–300 bids. Yahan se proof aur reviews lo, paisa nahi.

Social posts hot

LinkedIn, Facebook, X. Client khud "chahiye" likhta hai. Pehle 30 minute mein reply = 3x zyada reply rate.

No-website business best

Google Maps / Instagram. Client ne kabhi post hi nahi daala. Competition zero, poori price milti hai.

Rule: din ke 2 ghante = 40 min marketplace + 40 min social + 40 min direct outreach. 30 din mein 3–5 paying clients, agar follow-up chhoda nahi.

4Sabse zyada converting: jinke paas website hi nahi

9Red flags

🚩 Platform ke bahar WhatsApp

Freelancer par client bolta hai "WhatsApp karo" → account ban + payment scam. Platform ke andar hi rakho.

🚩 "Pehle free demo banao"

Max 1 screenshot ya 1-page mockup. Poori site free mein kabhi nahi.

🚩 "Payment baad mein, trust karo"

Escrow ya advance. Warna kaam nahi. Simple.

🚩 "Guaranteed #1 on Google"

Kabhi promise mat karo. SEO time leta hai — process batao, rank guarantee nahi.
